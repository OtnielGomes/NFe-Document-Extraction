# Cheap vision adopts gpt-6-luna with the structured prompts

Cheap vision now uses `gpt-6-luna` (Responses API, reasoning effort none, temperature 0) and the structured Header and Line items prompts. The comparison is the restarted sample of 20 synthetic examples, `synthetic-001` through `synthetic-010`, digital PDF then simulated photo. An earlier pass of steps 0–2 on all 200 examples was not used for this decision.

Step 0 (`nfe-cheap-vision-1e88b1ac`) re-ran `gpt-4.1-mini-2025-04-14` with the previous prompts: digital PDF field accuracy 0.9749, cost US$ 0.007262, 9 clean documents to Human review; simulated photo field accuracy 0.5822, 8 to Human review. Planted-error detection was 1.0 and clean documents rejected was 0 on both rows. Step 1 (`nfe-cheap-vision-053b570d`) changed only the model: digital review fell to 8 and cost to US$ 0.002086, photo field accuracy rose to 0.6168, and photo review rose to 9. Step 2 (`nfe-cheap-vision-417d12f6`) kept that model and used the new prompts: digital review stayed 8 at US$ 0.002035, photo field accuracy rose to 0.6420, and photo review stayed 9. Planted-error detection stayed 1.0 and no clean document was rejected. The new prompts on `gpt-4.1-mini-2025-04-14` (`nfe-cheap-vision-b90a3557`) cost US$ 0.007735 on digital PDF, sent 9 and 9 clean documents to Human review, and dropped photo field accuracy to 0.5590.

Simulated-photo Human review is 9 on both Luna runs and 8 on step 0, so that rendition does not meet Human review ≤ step 0. The mini prompt run misses the US$ 0.0071 digital cost cap, sends 9 digital and 9 photo documents to Human review, and drops photo field accuracy to 0.5590. Between the Luna runs, the review counts match and step 2 has the higher photo field accuracy, so that run is the adopted row in `docs/results.md`. Digital cost is under the cap, planted-error detection stays 1.0, and clean documents rejected stays 0. The other Extraction approaches stay on their previous snapshots.
